# Prompt2Spot — Agency, creator and affiliate program

## Agency outbound system

Target: 50 researched contacts per week, not 50 generic messages per day.

### Ideal agency profile

- 2–25 employees or a specialized freelancer collective.
- Produces recurring social content for at least five clients.
- Serves e-commerce, local services, real estate, hospitality or SaaS.
- Founder, creative director or operations lead is visible online.
- Already discusses AI, production capacity, margins or short-form video.
- Has public client work that can support a specific sample concept.

### Disqualifiers

- No visible client work or active site.
- Enterprise procurement process that exceeds the 90-day validation window.
- Seeks only free production or unlimited revisions.
- Requests deceptive deepfakes, unlicensed assets or unsupported identity guarantees.

## Personalized sample workflow

Spend no more than 10–15 minutes before a reply.

1. Select one public client post or product page.
2. Write a 2–3 sentence concept; do not render a complete unpaid client ad.
3. Record a 20–30-second Loom-style video:
   - first 5 seconds: their client/work
   - next 10 seconds: the specific repeatable-work observation
   - final 10 seconds: Prompt2Spot workflow and invitation
4. If they reply, create a watermarked proof scene or workflow demo with permission.

## Four-touch agency sequence

### Touch 1 — Email or LinkedIn

**Subject:** A repeatable video idea for {{client_or_vertical}}

Hi {{first_name}},

I saw the {{specific_campaign_or_client}} work your team produced for {{client}}. The format looks like something that needs frequent new hooks while the script, voice and production steps repeat.

We built Prompt2Spot to turn a structured client brief and source assets into one guided workflow: script, voice, visuals, captions and final render. I recorded a 27-second concept for how it could fit {{agency}}:

{{personalized_video_link}}

If the workflow is relevant, I can show the full production and actual run economics in 20 minutes.

— {{sender}}

### Touch 2 — Two business days later

Hi {{first_name}}, one detail I did not include in the first note: the useful agency feature is not “AI clips.” It is keeping the same brief and approval structure across client work.

Would a workflow demo using {{client_or_vertical}} be useful, or is video production not a current capacity constraint?

### Touch 3 — Five business days later

Hi {{first_name}},

Here is the 60-second version of the process: {{public_agency_demo_link}}

It shows the inputs, approval stages, output and cost record. If you would like to test it, we run a paid pilot with a defined number of videos and revisions; the pilot credit can be applied to an Agency plan if both sides continue.

### Touch 4 — Close the loop

I will close the loop after this. My assumption was that {{agency}} could use a repeatable path from client assets to short-form video without adding another manual handoff.

If that becomes relevant later, reply with “workflow” and I will send the latest agency demo.

### Reply handling

- **Interested:** book a 20-minute qualification/demo.
- **Not now:** ask permission for a 60-day follow-up.
- **Price request:** share a range only after volume, revisions, lip-sync and approval requirements are known.
- **Feature request:** label current, planned or unsupported; never imply a roadmap commitment.
- **No response:** stop after touch 4.

## Agency demo agenda

1. 3 minutes — current client-video volume and bottleneck.
2. 5 minutes — one relevant final output.
3. 7 minutes — brief, assets, stages, approvals and render.
4. 3 minutes — actual unit economics and revision boundary.
5. 2 minutes — agree on pilot, owner and success metric.

### Qualification questions

- How many client videos are delivered per month?
- Which steps consume the most human time?
- Which client assets must remain consistent?
- Who approves script, voice and visual direction?
- How many creative revisions are normal?
- When is lip-sync genuinely required?
- What margin or capacity improvement would make a pilot worthwhile?

### Paid pilot structure

- Fixed scope: 3–5 videos for one vertical.
- One agreed source-asset standard.
- One creative revision round per video; technical failures do not consume it.
- Standard and lip-sync videos priced separately.
- Pilot payment is required.
- If the agency starts a recurring plan within 14 days, apply an agreed portion of the pilot fee as account credit.
- Success metric is selected before production: work time, throughput, gross margin or approval cycle.

Do not publish the pilot price until billing, credits and revision entitlements match the offer.

## Creator and affiliate program

### Partner profiles

1. AI-workflow educators.
2. Social-media managers and consultants.
3. E-commerce creators.
4. Agency-growth operators.
5. No-code and small-business educators.

Start with creators whose audience is narrow and commercially relevant, even if they have only 5,000–50,000 followers.

### Founding-partner offer

Choose one compensation model per partner:

- **Revenue share:** 25%–30% of net collected revenue for the first three paid months of each referred customer.
- **Product credits:** higher face-value credits for creators who primarily want to demonstrate the workflow.
- **Hybrid test:** smaller revenue share plus credits, approved manually.

“Net collected revenue” excludes VAT/sales tax, refunds, chargebacks, payment fees and credited/free usage. Final terms require legal and tax review before public launch.

### Attribution rules

- Unique partner ID and landing URL.
- 30-day last-click attribution window.
- No self-referrals.
- No paid search bidding on Prompt2Spot brand terms or misspellings.
- No coupon sites, spam, forced clicks or misleading claims.
- Commission becomes payable after the refund/chargeback window.
- Manual review for duplicate accounts, unusual conversion patterns and related payment identities.

### Creator outreach message

**Subject:** A real Prompt2Spot workflow for your audience

Hi {{first_name}},

Your content about {{specific_topic}} is unusually practical — especially {{specific_post}}. We are building Prompt2Spot, a workflow that turns a business brief and source images into the script, voice, visual stages and final video.

I would like to give you a real account and let you test it with your own brief. We do not require a positive review or provide a fake testimonial script. If it is useful to your audience, the founding-partner program offers {{commission_or_credit_model}} with a tracked link.

Would you like to see a 45-second unedited workflow capture first?

### Creator brief

Required:

- Show the real input and final result.
- Disclose the commercial relationship.
- State when an output was edited outside Prompt2Spot.
- Use only licensed source assets.
- Send viewers to the unique tracked page.

Recommended:

- Lead with the result.
- Explain one concrete use case.
- Mention a real limitation or trade-off.
- Compare workflows, not cherry-picked model output.

Forbidden:

- Guaranteed earnings or performance claims.
- Undisclosed sponsorship/affiliate relationship.
- Fake customer or celebrity work.
- “Free” claims when payment is required.
- Product capabilities not available in the demonstrated account.

## Partner onboarding checklist

1. Approve partner and audience fit.
2. Sign terms and capture tax/payment details.
3. Issue unique partner ID, link and coupon if supported.
4. Grant product credits.
5. Provide:
   - 45-second demo
   - positioning guide
   - claim and disclosure rules
   - source-asset pack
   - three example briefs
   - FAQ and revision policy
6. Review the first asset for factual accuracy, not editorial positivity.
7. Track clicks, started creations, purchases, refunds and net contribution.
8. Review performance after 30 days.

## Weekly partner operating rhythm

- Monday: research 10 creators and 50 agency contacts.
- Tuesday–Thursday: send 10 personalized agency contacts/day and 3 creator contacts/day.
- Friday: follow-ups, demos, attribution review and partner-content amplification.

### 90-day targets

- Days 1–30: 200 agency contacts, 10 qualified replies, 4 demos.
- Days 31–60: 5 paid agency pilots, 10 creator partners onboarded.
- Days 61–90: 2 active agency partners, 5 active affiliates, one repeatable partner-sourced acquisition pattern.

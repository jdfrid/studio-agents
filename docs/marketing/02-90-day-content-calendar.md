# Prompt2Spot — 90-day content calendar

Primary language: English.  
Validation market: Hebrew versions of winning assets.  
Primary audiences: SMB owners and agencies/social-media managers.

## Weekly production system

Produce once, distribute many times.

### Monday — batch preparation (2–3 hours)

1. Select one real or portfolio business.
2. Define one offer, audience and source-asset set.
3. Generate one hero video and one alternate concept in Prompt2Spot.
4. Capture the brief, stage outputs, run cost and elapsed work time.
5. Cut the production into:
   - 2 outcome-first shorts
   - 1 prompt-to-result short
   - 1 educational short
   - 1 agency/operations short
   - 1 LinkedIn carousel or text post
   - 1 email

### Distribution cadence

| Day | Short-form | LinkedIn | YouTube | Email / community |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Final-result teaser | Founder observation | — | — |
| Tuesday | Prompt → result | Case-study post | Short | — |
| Wednesday | Educational / objection | Agency operations post | — | Community repost |
| Thursday | DIY comparison | — | Short | Weekly email |
| Friday | Alternate concept / trend | Weekly numbers or lesson | Tutorial every other week | Partner outreach asset |

Publish vertical video to TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Do not upload with another platform’s watermark. Adjust captions and opening text per channel instead of posting an identical wrapper.

## Content scorecard

Score every asset 48–72 hours after publication.

| Metric | Minimum signal | Strong signal | Action |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 3-second hold | 65% | 75%+ | Rewrite first frame below minimum |
| Completion on 20–30s video | 25% | 35%+ | Promote only strong assets |
| Engagement rate | 2% | 3%+ | Turn comments into follow-ups |
| Profile/site action | 0.5% | 1%+ | Reuse CTA and concept |
| Qualified replies | 1 | 3+ | Create industry-specific sequel |

## Four reusable short-form scripts

### Script A — Outcome first

**0–2s:** Show the finished scene. On-screen: “This started as three product photos.”  
**2–7s:** Show the source photos and one-line brief.  
**7–17s:** Fast stage sequence: script, voice, scenes, render.  
**17–25s:** Show final video highlights.  
**CTA:** “Watch the full prompt-to-result breakdown.”

### Script B — DIY comparison

**0–2s:** “Five AI tools. One 30-second ad.”  
**2–10s:** Show manual handoffs between script, voice, image, video and editor.  
**10–20s:** Show the Prompt2Spot stage flow.  
**20–27s:** Show both results and state the measured work time.  
**CTA:** “Choose the workflow you want to manage.”

### Script C — Agency

**0–2s:** “Agency owners: AI does not fix a broken production workflow.”  
**2–8s:** Show three different client briefs.  
**8–20s:** Show consistent stage structure and approvals.  
**20–27s:** Show three outputs.  
**CTA:** “Book the agency workflow demo.”

### Script D — Lip-sync economics

**0–3s:** “Do you actually need lip-sync in this ad?”  
**3–11s:** Show voice-over concept.  
**11–19s:** Show talking-character concept.  
**19–25s:** Explain that lip-sync adds cost and should serve the message.  
**CTA:** “See the cost breakdown before choosing.”

## Repurposing checklist

For every hero production, save:

- Original source assets and permission.
- Final 16:9 and 9:16 exports.
- Clean version without burned headline.
- Captions file and transcript.
- Screen recording of the workflow.
- Cost and work-time snapshot.
- Three still frames for carousels/thumbnails.
- UTM campaign name and content ID.
- Claim evidence file for any numeric statement.

# Prompt2Spot — Global positioning and creative kit

This document is the source of truth for English-language acquisition creative. Do not describe Prompt2Spot as “another AI video generator.” Sell the finished business outcome and the workflow it replaces.

## Message architecture

### Category

**AI business-video production workflow**

Prompt2Spot turns a brief and source images into a structured, publish-ready business video: script, voice, visual direction, generated scenes, captions, branding and final render.

### Core promise

**From business idea and product photos to a finished promotional video — without stitching together five AI tools.**

### Reasons to believe

1. One guided workflow from brief to render.
2. Product and character images can anchor the visual output.
3. Script, TTS, captions, branding and video are packaged together.
4. The user can approve stages or run automatically.
5. Cost and render activity are visible instead of hidden behind an opaque process.

### Claims that require proof before publication

Do not publish numeric claims without a measured case study.

- “X minutes from prompt to video”
- “Y% cheaper than an editor”
- “Z videos in one afternoon”
- Conversion, engagement or revenue uplift
- “Pixel-perfect” identity or guaranteed lip-sync

Use “designed to,” “typically,” or an actual measured range until at least 20 successful production runs support a stronger claim.

## Audience 1: SMB / business owner

### Job to be done

“I need a credible product or social video, but I do not want to learn prompting, editing, voice tools and rendering.”

### Primary message

**Your product. Your message. A finished video workflow.**

### Supporting messages

- Upload product photos, describe the offer and let one workflow handle the production steps.
- Start with a business brief, not a blank editing timeline.
- Produce repeatable social videos without rebuilding the process every week.
- Add lip-sync only when the concept needs it; do not pay for it by default.

### Objections and responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| “I can make AI videos myself.” | You can. Prompt2Spot is for businesses that value the finished workflow, consistent assets and saved operating time more than managing separate tools. |
| “Will it use my real product?” | Product images can be used as locked visual references; show a real before/after example rather than making an absolute guarantee. |
| “What if the result fails?” | Technical provider failures are retried; creative changes are handled as explicit revisions. Publish the exact policy on the pricing page. |
| “Is this another template maker?” | The workflow generates the script, voice, scenes and final render from the brief; it is not only a template library. |

## Audience 2: agency / social-media manager

### Job to be done

“I need to increase client-video output without adding editors or destroying margin.”

### Primary message

**Turn repeatable client briefs into a scalable video-production workflow.**

### Supporting messages

- Standardize brief → script → voice → visual → render across client accounts.
- Keep approval checkpoints where the client needs control.
- Reuse a structured process instead of rebuilding every production from scratch.
- Separate standard video economics from optional lip-sync economics.

### Objections and responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| “AI output is inconsistent.” | Lead with stage approvals, visual references and examples from the same industry. Do not promise universal consistency. |
| “We already use several AI tools.” | Position Prompt2Spot as workflow consolidation and operational leverage, not as a replacement for every specialist tool. |
| “Can we use it for multiple clients?” | The Agency offer should include client-level organization, clear credit allocation and a documented revision policy before scaling paid acquisition. |
| “Can we white-label it?” | Treat white-label as a sales conversation until the product and terms explicitly support it. |

## Creative pillars

Each pillar must generate both SMB and Agency variants.

1. **Outcome:** show the final video first.
2. **Transformation:** source photos and brief → finished result.
3. **Workflow:** show the stages replaced by one process.
4. **Economics:** measured time/cost comparison, never an invented claim.
5. **Control:** approvals, references, captions, branding and revision policy.
6. **Education:** how to brief an AI video so it looks intentional.
7. **Proof:** customer or industry case study.

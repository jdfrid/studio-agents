# Prompt2Spot — Paid media playbook

Monthly test budget: ₪2,000–₪4,000.  
Market: global, acquired sequentially — English-speaking markets first.  
Principle: paid media amplifies proven organic creative; it does not fund blind global awareness.

## Budget allocation

| Channel | Share | ₪2K month | ₪4K month | Purpose |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Meta | 45% | ₪900 | ₪1,800 | Creative discovery + retargeting |
| Google Search | 30% | ₪600 | ₪1,200 | Capture high-intent demand |
| Winning-content boosts | 15% | ₪300 | ₪600 | Amplify proven organic assets |
| Creator/partner tests | 10% | ₪200 | ₪400 | Referral and niche-audience tests |

Do not buy LinkedIn ads at this stage. Global SaaS CPC benchmarks are too high for the available budget. Use LinkedIn for founder content, agency outreach and retargetable site traffic generated elsewhere.

## Geographic rollout

The word “global” describes the reachable market, not the first ad set.

1. **Phase 1:** one English-speaking market or low-cost cluster selected from organic traffic.
2. **Phase 2:** add one geography only after five purchases or ten qualified agency leads from the existing message.
3. **Phase 3:** localize one proven creative system into one language at a time.

Starting options:

- United Kingdom + Ireland
- Australia + New Zealand
- English-language EMEA audiences
- United States only after a creative has proven conversion elsewhere; US CPC is usually more expensive

Israel remains a low-cost proof market with Hebrew creative, but results must not be blended with English campaigns.

## Meta campaign structure

### Campaign M1 — Creative validation

**Objective:** landing-page views or video engagement during the first 7–10 days; switch to purchase only after event volume is sufficient.

**Budget:** 60% of Meta allocation.

**Ad sets:**

1. SMB owners / e-commerce / local business.
2. Marketing freelancers / social-media managers.
3. Agency owners / small-agency employees.

Keep geography and placements identical while testing hooks. Do not split into tiny interest stacks.

**Creative cells:**

| Variable | Variants |
|---|---|
| Hook | Outcome / time saved / DIY comparison |
| Format | 9:16 video / 1:1 video |
| Audience | SMB / Agency |
| CTA | Watch demo / Create first video / Book workflow demo |

Start with six cells: three hooks × two audiences. Each cell gets one video; do not test six visual styles simultaneously.

### Campaign M2 — Retargeting

**Budget:** 40% of Meta allocation after the audience is large enough.

**Audiences:**

- 7-day pricing-page visitors: pricing proof + first-purchase CTA.
- 14-day product-demo viewers at 50%+: case study.
- 30-day site visitors: FAQ/objection creative.
- Started checkout but did not pay: revision policy, proof and direct return to checkout.
- Exclude paying customers from acquisition; place them in upsell/repeat-purchase messaging.

**Frequency guardrail:** investigate when 7-day frequency exceeds 3 and CTR declines.

## Google Search structure

Use exact and phrase match only during validation. Send SMB and Agency keywords to separate pages.

### Campaign G1 — Product-video intent

**Ad groups and starter keywords:**

- Product images:
  - `[create product video from photos]`
  - `"product photo to video ai"`
  - `[ai product ad generator]`
  - `"create product ad from images"`
- Business social video:
  - `[ai business video maker]`
  - `"ai video maker for small business"`
  - `[create social media ad video]`
- Full workflow:
  - `"ai video generator with voiceover"`
  - `"generate script voice and video"`

### Campaign G2 — Agency intent

- `[ai video tool for agencies]`
- `"agency video workflow"`
- `"create client videos with ai"`
- `[white label ai video platform]` — use only if the offer truly supports it
- `"bulk social media video creation"`

### Negative-keyword starter list

`free`, `download`, `crack`, `torrent`, `jobs`, `salary`, `course`, `prompt examples`, `anime`, `movie`, `NSFW`, `open source`, `GitHub`, `research paper`, `textbook`, `wedding invitation`, `video player`, `editor jobs`.

Review search terms twice weekly. Add negatives before raising bids.

## Winning-content boost rules

An organic asset qualifies for a boost only if it meets at least two:

- 3-second hold ≥75%.
- Completion ≥35% for 20–30-second video.
- Engagement ≥3%.
- Site/profile action ≥1%.
- At least three qualified comments or direct messages.

Boost with ₪30–₪60/day for three days. Keep the original post ID where possible to preserve social proof. Promote to the same market used for validation, not worldwide broad.

## Creator test rules

- Start with performance compensation, not fixed fees.
- Offer tracked commission or product credits.
- Provide a real account and example brief; do not provide a scripted fake testimonial.
- Require original screen/video demonstration.
- Give each creator a unique landing link and `utm_content`.

## Decision and stop rules

### Creative

- Stop or re-edit after 1,500–2,000 impressions if CTR and retention are both below target.
- If hold is weak, change the first 1–2 seconds, not the targeting.
- If retention is strong but clicks are weak, change CTA and offer.
- If clicks are strong but landing conversion is weak, do not scale the ad; fix message match and proof.

### Search

- Pause a keyword after 20–30 clicks with no meaningful activation unless search-term quality clearly justifies more data.
- Do not raise bids on keywords that produce signups but no completed video or purchase.
- Separate brand and non-brand results.

### Economics

- First-purchase CAC target: ≤₪100 during validation.
- Subscription CAC must pay back within three months of contribution margin.
- Never optimize to cheap leads alone; report qualified demo, purchase and repeat purchase.
- Increase a winning daily budget by no more than 20%–30% every 3–4 days.

## Weekly paid-media review

1. Spend by market, audience and creative.
2. Search terms and negative-keyword additions.
3. 3-second hold, completion, CTR and landing conversion.
4. Cost per started creation and completed creation.
5. Cost per first purchase.
6. Repeat purchase and subscription conversion by acquisition source.
7. Gross contribution after actual run costs.
8. Decision: stop, repair, continue or scale.
